# Northwind Gear Co. — Product FAQ (Excerpt)

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the continental US and is free on orders over $75. Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) costs $14.99 flat rate. We do not currently ship outside the US and Canada.

## Returns
Unworn items with tags attached can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Worn or washed items are only eligible for store credit, issued at 70% of the original price. Sale items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

## Warranty
All Northwind hardshell backpacks come with a 2-year manufacturer's warranty covering zipper failure, seam separation, and buckle breakage. The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, fading from UV exposure, or damage from checked airline baggage handling.

## Sizing
Our jackets run true to US sizing. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a layering-friendly fit. Customers who have purchased both S and M in the same style report the M as noticeably roomier in the shoulders.

## Loyalty Program
Northwind Rewards members earn 1 point per $1 spent. 500 points converts to a $25 credit. Points expire 18 months after the account's last purchase. Birthday month members get a one-time 15% discount code.
