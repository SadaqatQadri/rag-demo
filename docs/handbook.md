# Northwind Gear Co. — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval. Remote days must be logged in the HR portal by Friday of the prior week. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed every 6 months.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of PTO per month, capped at 18 days per calendar year. Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next year; anything beyond that is forfeited on December 31st. PTO requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance through the HR portal, except in documented emergencies.

## Expense Reimbursement
Employees are reimbursed for pre-approved business expenses within 30 days of submitting a receipt. Expenses over $200 require manager pre-approval before the purchase is made. Reimbursements are processed on the 15th and last day of each month via direct deposit.

## Equipment Policy
New employees receive a standard equipment package (laptop, monitor, headset) within their first week. Equipment requests outside the standard package (e.g., a second monitor, ergonomic chair) go through the IT ticketing system and are approved by the employee's manager.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 12 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are eligible for 4 weeks of paid leave. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption date and can be split into two blocks with manager agreement.
